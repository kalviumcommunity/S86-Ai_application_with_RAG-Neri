Hydraulic Press Maintenance Manual
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Hydraulic Press
Machine ID: PRS-001
Manufacturer: Schuler
Model: HP-160
Department: Forming
Location: Plant A - Bay 4

General Troubleshooting Guidance
--------------------------------
Use this manual together with approved maintenance logs and safety procedures.
Technicians must stop the machine before performing inspection or maintenance.
Do not perform procedures that are not supported by approved documentation.

Problem: low pressure
---------------------
1. Stop the press before performing inspection.
2. Check the hydraulic fluid level.
3. Inspect accessible hydraulic connections for visible leakage.
4. Review maintenance history for previous pressure problems.

Problem: pressure fluctuation
-----------------------------
1. Stop operation if pressure becomes unstable.
2. Check hydraulic fluid level according to the manual.
3. Review the maintenance log for previous pressure fluctuations.
4. Inspect accessible hydraulic lines for visible problems.

Problem: hydraulic leak
-----------------------
1. Stop the machine before inspecting the hydraulic system.
2. Identify the visible location of the leak without contacting hydraulic fluid.
3. Review maintenance records for previous hydraulic leaks.
4. Do not open pressurized hydraulic components unless authorized.

Problem: slow operation
-----------------------
1. Stop the press if movement is significantly slower than normal.
2. Check hydraulic fluid level.
3. Review maintenance records for previous slow-operation events.
4. Inspect accessible hydraulic lines for visible leakage.

Problem: unexpected shutdown
----------------------------
1. Record the machine alarm or error code.
2. Keep the press in a safe stopped condition.
3. Review recent maintenance records.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Problem: overheating
--------------------
1. Stop the press and allow the system to cool.
2. Check hydraulic fluid level.
3. Inspect accessible cooling components.
4. Review maintenance history for previous overheating events.

Escalation Guidance
-------------------
If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, stop further intervention and escalate the problem to the authorized maintenance supervisor.
