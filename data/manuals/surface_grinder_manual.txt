Surface Grinder Maintenance Manual
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Surface Grinder
Machine ID: GRD-001
Manufacturer: Okamoto
Model: ACC-63
Department: Finishing
Location: Plant B - Bay 5

General Troubleshooting Guidance
--------------------------------
Use this manual together with approved maintenance logs and safety procedures.
Technicians must stop the machine before performing inspection or maintenance.
Do not perform procedures that are not supported by approved documentation.

Problem: grinding wheel vibration
---------------------------------
1. Stop the grinder before inspection.
2. Inspect the grinding wheel for visible damage.
3. Check the wheel mounting condition according to the approved procedure.
4. Review maintenance records for previous vibration issues.

Problem: poor surface finish
----------------------------
1. Stop the operation if surface quality deteriorates.
2. Inspect the grinding wheel condition.
3. Review recent maintenance history.
4. Verify coolant flow according to the machine procedure.

Problem: wheel wear
-------------------
1. Stop the machine before inspecting the wheel.
2. Inspect the wheel condition.
3. Review maintenance records.
4. Replace the wheel only according to the approved procedure.

Problem: coolant flow issue
---------------------------
1. Stop grinding before inspecting the coolant system.
2. Check coolant level.
3. Inspect accessible coolant lines.
4. Review previous maintenance records.

Problem: motor overheating
--------------------------
1. Stop the grinder and allow the motor to cool.
2. Check ventilation around the motor.
3. Review maintenance history.
4. Do not access internal electrical components while energized.

Problem: unexpected shutdown
----------------------------
1. Record the alarm or error code.
2. Keep the machine stopped.
3. Review recent maintenance records.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Escalation Guidance
-------------------
If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, stop further intervention and escalate the problem to the authorized maintenance supervisor.
