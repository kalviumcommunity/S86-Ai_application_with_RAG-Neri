Robotic Welding Cell Maintenance Manual
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Robotic Welding Cell
Machine ID: ROB-001
Manufacturer: ABB
Model: IRB 2600
Department: Welding
Location: Plant C - Bay 5

General Troubleshooting Guidance
--------------------------------
Use this manual together with approved maintenance logs and safety procedures.
Technicians must stop the machine before performing inspection or maintenance.
Do not perform procedures that are not supported by approved documentation.

Problem: welding arc unstable
-----------------------------
1. Stop the welding operation before inspection.
2. Inspect accessible welding consumables.
3. Check the maintenance log for previous arc stability issues.
4. Follow the approved welding-system inspection procedure.

Problem: robot movement error
-----------------------------
1. Stop the robot before inspection.
2. Record the controller alarm.
3. Review recent maintenance records.
4. Do not manually move the robot outside the approved procedure.

Problem: wire feeding issue
---------------------------
1. Stop the welding operation.
2. Inspect the accessible wire-feed path.
3. Review maintenance records for previous wire-feed problems.
4. Replace consumables according to the approved procedure.

Problem: excessive vibration
----------------------------
1. Stop the robot before inspection.
2. Inspect accessible tooling and fixtures.
3. Review previous maintenance records.
4. Do not enter the robot work envelope without authorization.

Problem: controller alarm
-------------------------
1. Record the exact controller alarm.
2. Keep the robot stopped.
3. Review maintenance history for the alarm.
4. Follow the approved controller recovery procedure.

Problem: unexpected shutdown
----------------------------
1. Record the alarm or error code.
2. Keep the welding cell stopped.
3. Review recent maintenance records.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Escalation Guidance
-------------------
If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, stop further intervention and escalate the problem to the authorized maintenance supervisor.
