Air Compressor Maintenance Manual
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Air Compressor
Machine ID: CMP-001
Manufacturer: Atlas Copco
Model: GA 30
Department: Utilities
Location: Plant D - Utility Room

General Troubleshooting Guidance
--------------------------------
Use this manual together with approved maintenance logs and safety procedures.
Technicians must stop the machine before performing inspection or maintenance.
Do not perform procedures that are not supported by approved documentation.

Problem: low air pressure
-------------------------
1. Check the pressure reading and record the observed value.
2. Inspect accessible air lines for visible leakage.
3. Review maintenance records for previous pressure problems.
4. Check the compressor operating status according to the manual.

Problem: high discharge temperature
-----------------------------------
1. Stop the compressor and allow it to cool.
2. Check ventilation around the compressor.
3. Review maintenance history for previous temperature problems.
4. Do not access hot internal components while the compressor is operating.

Problem: oil leak
-----------------
1. Stop the compressor before inspection.
2. Identify the visible location of the leak without contacting hot or pressurized components.
3. Review maintenance records for previous oil leaks.
4. Do not open pressurized components unless authorized.

Problem: abnormal noise
-----------------------
1. Stop the compressor if abnormal noise increases.
2. Record when the noise occurs.
3. Inspect accessible external components.
4. Review maintenance records for similar noise reports.

Problem: compressor not starting
--------------------------------
1. Check the control panel for alarms.
2. Confirm the compressor is in the correct operating state.
3. Review recent maintenance records.
4. Follow the approved startup procedure.

Problem: unexpected shutdown
----------------------------
1. Record the alarm or error code.
2. Keep the compressor stopped.
3. Review maintenance history.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Escalation Guidance
-------------------
If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, stop further intervention and escalate the problem to the authorized maintenance supervisor.
