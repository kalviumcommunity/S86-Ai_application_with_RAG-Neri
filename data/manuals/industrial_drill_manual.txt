Industrial Drill Maintenance Manual
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Industrial Drill
Machine ID: DRL-001
Manufacturer: Bosch Rexroth
Model: ID-500
Department: Fabrication
Location: Plant B - Bay 1

General Troubleshooting Guidance
--------------------------------
Use this manual together with approved maintenance logs and safety procedures.
Technicians must stop the machine before performing inspection or maintenance.
Do not perform procedures that are not supported by approved documentation.

Problem: drill bit slipping
---------------------------
1. Stop the drill before inspecting the tool holder.
2. Check the drill bit and chuck for visible damage.
3. Verify that the drill bit is secured according to the operating procedure.
4. Review maintenance records for previous chuck problems.

Problem: excessive vibration
----------------------------
1. Stop drilling before inspection.
2. Inspect the drill bit for damage or excessive wear.
3. Check the workpiece setup.
4. Review previous vibration-related maintenance records.

Problem: motor overheating
--------------------------
1. Stop the drill and allow the motor to cool.
2. Check for blocked ventilation around the motor.
3. Review maintenance records for previous overheating events.
4. Do not access internal electrical components while energized.

Problem: poor drilling accuracy
-------------------------------
1. Stop the operation if accuracy continues to degrade.
2. Inspect the drill bit for wear.
3. Check the workpiece setup.
4. Review maintenance history for alignment problems.

Problem: abnormal noise
-----------------------
1. Stop the drill if abnormal noise increases.
2. Record when the noise occurs.
3. Inspect accessible tooling for visible damage.
4. Review previous maintenance records.

Problem: unexpected shutdown
----------------------------
1. Record the displayed alarm.
2. Keep the machine stopped until the cause is reviewed.
3. Check recent maintenance records.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Escalation Guidance
-------------------
If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, stop further intervention and escalate the problem to the authorized maintenance supervisor.
