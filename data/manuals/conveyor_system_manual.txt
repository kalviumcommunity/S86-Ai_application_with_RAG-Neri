Conveyor System Maintenance Manual
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Conveyor System
Machine ID: CON-001
Manufacturer: Dorner
Model: 2200 Series
Department: Material Handling
Location: Plant C - Bay 1

General Troubleshooting Guidance
--------------------------------
Use this manual together with approved maintenance logs and safety procedures.
Technicians must stop the machine before performing inspection or maintenance.
Do not perform procedures that are not supported by approved documentation.

Problem: belt slipping
----------------------
1. Stop the conveyor before inspection.
2. Inspect the belt and accessible drive components.
3. Check the maintenance log for previous belt-slip events.
4. Adjust or repair the belt only according to the approved procedure.

Problem: belt misalignment
--------------------------
1. Stop the conveyor before inspection.
2. Inspect belt tracking and accessible rollers.
3. Review previous maintenance records.
4. Correct alignment according to the approved procedure.

Problem: motor overheating
--------------------------
1. Stop the conveyor and allow the motor to cool.
2. Check ventilation around the motor.
3. Review maintenance history.
4. Do not access internal electrical components while energized.

Problem: abnormal noise
-----------------------
1. Stop the conveyor if the noise is unexpected or increasing.
2. Inspect accessible rollers and belt components.
3. Review maintenance records.
4. Do not enter guarded areas without following safety procedures.

Problem: conveyor not starting
------------------------------
1. Confirm the conveyor is in a safe stopped state.
2. Check the operator panel for alarms.
3. Review recent maintenance records.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Problem: unexpected shutdown
----------------------------
1. Record the alarm or error condition.
2. Keep the conveyor stopped until the cause is identified.
3. Review maintenance history.
4. Follow the approved restart procedure.

Escalation Guidance
-------------------
If the documented troubleshooting steps do not resolve the issue, stop further intervention and escalate the problem to the authorized maintenance supervisor.
