Milling Machine Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Milling Machine
Machine ID: MIL-001
Manufacturer: Bridgeport
Model: Series 1
Location: Plant B - Bay 3

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Stop the spindle before inspecting tooling.
2. Keep hands away from rotating components.
3. Use lockout and tagout before authorized maintenance.
4. Do not bypass machine guards or safety interlocks.
5. Follow approved emergency procedures for smoke, fire, or electrical faults.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
