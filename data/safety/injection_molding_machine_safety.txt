Injection Molding Machine Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Injection Molding Machine
Machine ID: IMM-001
Manufacturer: Engel
Model: Victory 330
Location: Plant C - Bay 3

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Keep clear of the mold closing area.
2. Use lockout and tagout before maintenance inside hazardous areas.
3. Do not touch hot surfaces or molten material.
4. Never bypass machine safety interlocks.
5. Follow emergency procedures for hydraulic leaks, fire, smoke, or electrical faults.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
