Conveyor System Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Conveyor System
Machine ID: CON-001
Manufacturer: Dorner
Model: 2200 Series
Location: Plant C - Bay 1

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Never reach into a moving conveyor.
2. Stop the conveyor before inspecting belts or rollers.
3. Use lockout and tagout before entering guarded maintenance areas.
4. Do not bypass emergency stops or safety guards.
5. Follow emergency procedures for electrical faults, fire, or entrapment hazards.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
