Industrial Drill Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Industrial Drill
Machine ID: DRL-001
Manufacturer: Bosch Rexroth
Model: ID-500
Location: Plant B - Bay 1

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Stop the drill before changing or inspecting tooling.
2. Keep loose clothing and hands away from rotating components.
3. Use approved eye protection in the drilling area.
4. Use lockout and tagout before maintenance requiring access to hazardous components.
5. If electrical smoke or sparking occurs, stop operation and follow the emergency procedure.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
