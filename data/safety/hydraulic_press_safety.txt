Hydraulic Press Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Hydraulic Press
Machine ID: PRS-001
Manufacturer: Schuler
Model: HP-160
Location: Plant A - Bay 4

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Never place hands or body parts inside the press working area.
2. Use the approved lockout and tagout procedure before maintenance.
3. Do not inspect pressurized hydraulic components while the system is energized.
4. Keep unauthorized personnel away from the press during maintenance.
5. If a hydraulic leak creates a hazardous condition, stop operation and follow site safety procedures.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
