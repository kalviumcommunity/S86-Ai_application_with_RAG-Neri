Surface Grinder Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Surface Grinder
Machine ID: GRD-001
Manufacturer: Okamoto
Model: ACC-63
Location: Plant B - Bay 5

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Stop the grinder before inspecting the grinding wheel.
2. Do not operate the machine with a damaged grinding wheel.
3. Use approved eye and face protection.
4. Use lockout and tagout before authorized maintenance.
5. Follow emergency procedures if wheel damage, fire, smoke, or sparking occurs.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
