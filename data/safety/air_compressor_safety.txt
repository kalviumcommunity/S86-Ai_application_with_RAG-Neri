Air Compressor Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Air Compressor
Machine ID: CMP-001
Manufacturer: Atlas Copco
Model: GA 30
Location: Plant D - Utility Room

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Stop and isolate the compressor before authorized maintenance.
2. Do not open pressurized components while pressure remains in the system.
3. Keep clear of hot surfaces and moving components.
4. Use the approved lockout and tagout procedure.
5. Follow emergency procedures for fire, electrical faults, or significant air-system leaks.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
