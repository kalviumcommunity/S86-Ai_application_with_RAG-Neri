CNC Mill Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: CNC Mill
Machine ID: CNC-001
Manufacturer: Haas
Model: VF-2
Location: Plant A - Bay 1

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Stop the CNC mill before performing maintenance or inspection.
2. Do not inspect internal electrical components while the machine is connected to power.
3. Follow the organization's lockout and tagout procedure before accessing hazardous internal components.
4. Keep the machine area clear of unauthorized personnel during maintenance.
5. If smoke, fire, sparking, or electrical damage is observed, follow the approved emergency procedure.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
