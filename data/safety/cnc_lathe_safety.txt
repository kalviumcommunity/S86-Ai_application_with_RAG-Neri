CNC Lathe Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: CNC Lathe
Machine ID: LAT-001
Manufacturer: DMG Mori
Model: NLX 2500
Location: Plant A - Bay 2

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Stop the spindle before inspecting the chuck or workholding system.
2. Keep hands away from rotating components.
3. Use the approved lockout and tagout procedure before maintenance requiring access to hazardous components.
4. Do not bypass machine safety interlocks.
5. If electrical sparking, smoke, or fire occurs, follow the approved emergency procedure.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
