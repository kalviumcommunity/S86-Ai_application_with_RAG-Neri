Robotic Welding Cell Safety Procedures
==================================================

Machine Information
-------------------
Machine: Robotic Welding Cell
Machine ID: ROB-001
Manufacturer: ABB
Model: IRB 2600
Location: Plant C - Bay 5

Approved Safety Procedures
--------------------------
1. Do not enter the robot work envelope while the robot is enabled.
2. Use the approved lockout and tagout procedure before authorized maintenance.
3. Follow welding fume and eye-protection requirements.
4. Do not bypass robot safety interlocks.
5. Follow emergency procedures for electrical faults, fire, or uncontrolled robot movement.

Emergency Escalation
--------------------
For fire, smoke, electrical sparking, serious injury, uncontrolled machine movement, or another immediate hazard, stop operation when it is safe to do so and follow the organization's approved emergency procedure.

Safety Note
-----------
Safety procedures in this document are machine-specific approved guidance. Do not substitute undocumented procedures.
